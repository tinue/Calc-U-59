# Privacy Policy — Calc-U-59

**Last updated: 2026-10-19**

Calc-U-59 is a cycle-accurate emulator of three famous programmable calculators of the late 1970s and early 1980s. This policy covers the macOS, iOS and iPadOS app and the Windows and Linux app.

## Data Collection

Calc-U-59 does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

- No analytics or crash reporting is sent to the developer.
- No advertising identifiers or tracking technologies are used.
- No account registration or login is required or offered.
- The app makes no network connections.

## iCloud

If you have iCloud Drive enabled on your device, iOS and macOS may sync calculator card files (`.U59`) and the TI-58C constant memory file (`ti58c.mem`), stored in the app's iCloud container, to your other devices. This sync happens entirely between your devices via your personal iCloud account. The developer has no access to these files.

You can disable iCloud sync for Calc-U-59 at any time in **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud**.

## Windows and Linux

The Windows and Linux app does not use iCloud or any other cloud service. It
stores card files and the TI-58C constant memory file in a local folder
(`Calc-U-59` in your home folder by default, changeable in Settings).
